Sand: privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

This policy covers the Sand website at av.github.io/safe-sand and the Sand app for Android (package io.github.av.sand).

What Sand itself collects

Nothing. Sand has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your settings (sand type, screen, sound, haptics, graphics quality) and whether you own Sand Pro are stored only on your device.

Sand reads the motion sensor so the sand can follow the tilt of your phone. Motion data is used on the device in real time and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Ads in the Android app

The free Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the start screen and in the menu, and video ads that you choose to watch to try the extra sands. Sand Pro removes all ads. The website shows no ads.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ad interactions, and diagnostic data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Sand asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy choices" in the menu. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Purchases

Sand Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Payments privacy notice; Sand only learns whether you own Sand Pro.

Children

Sand is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/av/safe-sand/issues.
